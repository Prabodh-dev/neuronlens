# Auto-Lab: one-button steering test for a goal
Owner: Anand (UI); Mirdul (server: `with_activations` on the sweep, `server/scripts/autolab_check.py`, the mock replay)
Branch: anand (UI), mirdul (server)
User sees: its own page, "Auto-Lab" in the navbar (`/autolab`). Three numbered steps: pick a prompt (3 buttons), pick a goal (6 buttons, or type 1 to 3 words), run. While it runs ("about 20 seconds"): "Trying feature 2 of 5…" with a bar, the seconds so far, Cancel, live ✓ / ✗ / ⚠ counters, a short log of each feature's outcome, and the grid filling in live, slowly, cell by cell from left to right; the finished table plays one slow wave, row by row, when it is on screen. The grid: "features that fired on these words" (each with its name and the words around the peak of its top example) down the side; an "unsteered" control column with each feature's reading on the unsteered text (strength 0, shown, not tagged); then the 6 strengths, each ✓ worked, ✗ no effect or ⚠ breaks down, neighbouring ✓ joined by a line (stable); and "Breaks down from". Then: the counters; the verdict (a recommended test, "Worked at one strength only, not reliable", or "Nothing worked for this goal on this prompt"); and an inspector on the recommended test (its feature, strength and tag, its reading unsteered → steered counting up, which parts of the rule it met including stability, why it was picked, the loop reason for ⚠, a bar per strength after the unsteered control), with "Show text" (closed by default), "Open in Compare" and Copy link (S5). Every cell and bar can be clicked to inspect that test. In the site's black and white, tags differ by brightness and texture: ✓ bright white (the recommended test a solid white tile), ⚠ hatched with a dashed edge, ✗ dim grey; the accent is the site's grey-to-white heat ramp. Motion is one easing throughout and is off with reduced motion.
Endpoint / data used: existing endpoints only, every call one after another (one model lock), seed 42, 60 tokens.
1. `POST /api/v1/features/search` with " " + the goal's words (a leading space, so the first word gets the token it has mid-sentence), `max_density: 0.01`, `top_n: 8`. The 9 fillers are dropped (features that come back for many unrelated words, for example #1375 more – text navigation, #5023 off-, #3356 arden – website domains; `web/src/lib/autolab-fillers.json`, an unchanged copy of Mirdul's `demo/autolab_fillers.json`, measured on Prabodh's laptop with `--find-fillers`) and the first 5 kept as the candidates. Fewer than 3 left: "No feature matches these words. Try another word." and no sweep. Then `GET /features/{id}` for each candidate's name and top example (database reads).
2. For each candidate, `POST /api/v1/steer/sweep` with `with_activations: true`, the chosen prompt, and the Every strength tab's strengths (0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2; 0 is the unsteered control). Each strength gives its text, its loop tag and `target_activation_baseline` / `target_activation_steered`.
3. On a server whose sweep does not return those readings, `POST /api/v1/steer/check` (X1) supplies them for each strength that differs and does not loop.
Presets: `web/src/lib/autolab-presets.ts`. Prompts: "The band walked on stage and", "The man walked into the room and", "She opened the door and". Goals (from the laptop test; the words are those recorded in `demo/autolab_examples.json`): Music ("music"), Courts and law ("court law"), Space ("space planet"), Food ("cooking food"), Weather ("weather rain"), Sports ("sports game"). All 6 ship (Prabodh, after the laptop test). Run, tags and rule: `web/src/lib/autolab.ts`. Blocklist: `web/src/lib/autolab-blocklist.ts`. Fillers: `web/src/lib/autolab-fillers.json` (read by `autolab-fillers.ts`; the Features page "Your words" drops them too). Screen: `web/src/app/autolab/`.
Thresholds (`WORKED` in `web/src/lib/autolab.ts`, the same as `RISE_MIN` and `RATIO_MIN` in `server/scripts/autolab_check.py`; provisional until frozen). Rise = steered reading − unsteered reading.
- ✓ worked: rise ≥ 3.0 and steered ≥ 2× unsteered, and the loop tag is off. With the unsteered reading near 0, the 2× part holds by itself, so the rise decides.
- ✗ no effect: not worked, and the loop tag is off.
- ⚠ breaks down: the sweep's loop tag is on.
Recommendation (rule v2, as `recommend()` in `server/scripts/autolab_check.py`), strength 0 left out. A ✓ cell is stable if the strength next to it (before or after) is also ✓. Only features with at least one stable ✓ can be recommended; among them the one with the most ✓ cells (tie: the higher rise at its best ✓ cell, then search order), at its lowest stable ✓ strength, worded "tuned for this goal on this prompt". ✓ cells but none stable: "Worked at one strength only, not reliable", no recommendation. No ✓: "Nothing worked for this goal on this prompt". Checked: on the 18 recorded laptop runs (6 goals × 3 prompts), the UI's rule gives the same verdict as the script for every one.
Typed goal: 1 to 3 words, trimmed; Run is disabled while the box is empty and locked during a run. A word on the blocklist shows "Try another word" and runs nothing. After the search, when the query tokens it reports divided by the words typed (split on spaces) is more than 2 (`MAX_PIECES_PER_WORD`), the screen shows "The model splits these words into small pieces, so no feature matches them. Try a common word." with the pieces, and runs no sweep: random letters split into 3 to 4 pieces a word, real words and the 6 buttons into 1 to 1.5 (Prabodh's measurements; score thresholds cannot tell them apart). When fewer than 3 features are left after the fillers are dropped, the screen shows "No feature matches these words. Try another word." and runs no sweep.
Real demo example (feature ids, prompt, strength): Music on "The band walked on stage and": #3482 (guitar – or musical instrument) up 0.25, 2.93 → 9.73, 5 of 6 strengths ✓ (recorded on Prabodh's laptop with this search, `demo/autolab_examples.json` on Mirdul's branch).
Honest limit shown on screen: "A test on this prompt, not a fix." (always visible), with the seed, the length, and that the candidates are up to 5 features that fired most on the goal's words, leaving out the 9 that fire on almost any words. On a mock or sample-data server the run works and is labelled Simulated, in the header and under the result (the mock replays the recorded laptop runs for the preset goals; anything else gets stand-ins); with `NEXT_PUBLIC_MODEL_ONLY=1`, as on the demo machine, the whole app shows "Not running on the model" instead.
What it does not claim: a ✓ means the feature's reading on the finished text rose clearly without looping, on this prompt, with this seed. It does not say the text is better, that a feature stands for the goal, or that the result holds on other prompts or seeds. The candidates are features that fired on the goal's words, never "the feature for" the goal. "Breaks down" catches repetition, not every kind of poor text. Looping texts can be harsh, so all text stays behind "Show text".
Timing: the screen says "about 20 seconds" (`AUTOLAB_ETA_S` in `web/src/lib/autolab.ts`): timed UI runs on Prabodh's laptop took 20 to 25 s, against about 14 s for Mirdul's script on the same chain. Part of the gap was the UI reading each candidate's name and example one after another before the first sweep, each GET with a CORS preflight; those reads now run beside the sweeps and GETs send no preflight. The label is lowered only after real runs are timed again.
Status: checked against Mirdul's branch (re-recorded laptop runs and the 9 fillers): on all 18 recorded runs, live on his mock server, the UI and `server/scripts/autolab_check.py` agree on every candidate, every cell's tag and every verdict. His recordings and fillers file reach main with his next PR.
